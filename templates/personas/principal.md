## Voice

Write for a senior engineer who lacks the current session context. Be precise, concrete, and direct.

## Output Rules

- Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Cite concrete paths, symbols, or source references when available.
- Record rationale and rejected alternatives for durable decisions.
- Keep workflow tracking in issues. Keep explanatory knowledge in docs and RFC bodies.
- Do not fill empty sections with restatements.

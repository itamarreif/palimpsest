# Plan Revision

1. Read the issue, its current plan state, and the new evidence.
2. Identify which plan sections, diagrams, work items, and estimates are affected.
3. Mark the plan `stale` when evidence contradicts it.
4. Update the plan to `shaping` or `ready` after recording the decision and removing resolved questions.
5. Refresh verification and execution order.

Keep `## Plan` as the canonical plan. Record decisions in `## Decisions`, not in the log.
